The game combines grid-based turn-based combat, party management, corporate satire, relationship building, and time-based decision making.

## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Ledgerbound**. This trainer enhances Ledgerbound's single-player campaign by providing control over combat survivability, ability usage, burnout, time management, character progression, equipment bonuses, and relationship progress. It is designed for players who want to explore the story, experiment with party builds, or reduce the pressure of permanent choices and limited time.

**Target Audience:** Players who want a more relaxed story experience, easier tactical encounters, faster character development, flexible relationship progression, or freedom to experiment with party builds and corporate equipment.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Ledgerbound uses grid-based tactical encounters rather than continuous action movement. Activate combat-related options after entering a battle and select the desired party members before moving them across the battlefield. Avoid enabling trainer options during scene transitions or loading screens.

### Tools & Inventory

Corporate swag can be equipped to improve character statistics. Open the equipment or inventory interface, select the desired item, and activate the equipment enhancement option only after the inventory has finished loading. Review the updated values before leaving the menu.

### World & Environment

The campaign combines narrative scenes, insurance-related objectives, party recruitment, time management, and relationship decisions. Use the time and burnout options when planning evening activities or exploring alternate choices. Save before major decisions if you want to compare different outcomes.

### Combat & Weapons

Enter a tactical battle, allow the battlefield and party interface to finish loading, then activate survivability or ability options. Use the trainer to test different abilities, promotion paths, and party combinations without the usual combat penalties. Disable combat options before returning to story scenes or the main menu.
